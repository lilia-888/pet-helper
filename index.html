Guidance

What To Do If Your Pet Is Missing

  • 1 Post on this site right away so neighbors can see the alert
  • 2 Share the post on social media to reach more people
  • 3 Check local animal shelters — call or visit in person
  • 4 Notify your neighbors in person or in a local group chat
  • 5 Put up printed flyers in your neighborhood with a clear photo
  • 6 Check back on this site often — someone may report a sighting
For neighbors

How to Help a Neighbor's Lost Pet

What to do

  • Click "Contact Owner" on the pet card to message the owner
  • Take a photo if it is safe to do so
  • Write down the exact street or landmark where you saw the animal

What not to do

  • Do not chase the animal — it may run further away
  • Do not pick up the animal unless it is clearly injured

If the animal seems hurt

  • Contact a local animal shelter or animal control right away
  • Stay nearby if it is safe — do not leave an injured animal alone
How it works

Tips and How to Use This Site

How to search for a lost pet

Go to the Lost Pets tab and type a pet name, type, or area into the search boxes. Enter your location and a radius to see only nearby listings on the map.

How to post a missing pet

Go to the Report a Pet tab, fill in the form, and click "Report a Lost Pet." You need to be logged in to post.

How to contact an owner

Click "Contact Owner" on any pet card to open a private chat. You need to be logged in to send messages.

How to mark your pet as found

If your pet is home safe, click "Mark as Reunited" on your card. This removes the post so everyone knows the good news.
